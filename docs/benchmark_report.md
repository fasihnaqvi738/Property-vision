The ARKitScenes depth report is `outputs/arkitscenes_public_sample/arkitscenes_depth_evaluation.json`; its capture is `captures/_public_arkitscenes/raw/Training/42445884` and its local manifest records SHA-256 hashes. The reference is generated by projecting a FARO-derived mesh into the wide-camera view. Frame timing, normalized raster resizing, and the reference projection are assumptions; this pixel metric is **not** a floor-plan or room-dimension score and is not compared with the assessment's 1.5 cm ceiling-height gate.

The apartment reference drawing has a separate manual trace with 8 primary rooms/lobby, 5 ancillary spaces, 2 connectors, and 14 adjacency annotations. Per user instruction, its 11 complete printed room-dimension pairs and one partial pair are authoritative scoring targets for this apartment. There is no independent tape/laser cross-check; the pixel-coordinate trace remains separate from sensor predictions. These targets do not generalize to the unseen walk-in property.

An additional scale audit checked whether the 11 fully dimensioned, manually traced polygons could share one pixel-to-metre mapping. Even after assigning each printed width/length pair to the better of the two axis orientations and fitting separate X/Y scales, the best RMS dimension residual is 27.6%. The printed labels remain authoritative per-room targets, but the hand-traced polygon coordinates cannot be promoted to a metric stitched plan. The reproducible diagnostic is `pipeline/evaluation/audit_reference_plan_scale.py`; its local output is `outputs/apartment_case_01/stitched_plan/plan_scale_audit.json`.

The photo registration change has a replayable diagnostic bundle at `benchmark/photo_registration_fix/`: its largest sparse model increased from 8/23 to 20/23 photos on the saved listing-photo input. The bundle includes path-free result projections, SHA-256 provenance, before/after reports, and a fix-loop report. The diagnostic target moved in the desired direction, but its declared 100% operational target was not reached; more importantly, the official measured whole-property stitch gate remains **NOT SCORED** because sparse camera registration does not provide a floor plan or measured truth.

## Reproduction

Run `python main.py "captures\\c00a170fe1" --output-dir outputs` for the supplied RGB-D sample. Run `python main.py "captures\\apartment_case_01\\photo_tier" --output-dir outputs` for the apartment still collection and `python main.py "captures\\apartment_case_01\\apartment_video.mp4" --video-segments-json "captures\\apartment_case_01\\video_room_segments.json" --output-dir outputs` for the reviewed video intervals. To reproduce the external LiDAR sensor-depth check, download sample `42445884` with `python -m pipeline.evaluation.download_arkitscenes_sample --output-dir "captures\\_public_arkitscenes"`, run `main.py` on `captures\\_public_arkitscenes\\raw\\Training\\42445884`, then run `python -m pipeline.evaluation.evaluate_arkitscenes_depth` on that capture folder with an output path. The download is about 161 MB compressed and its media stays in ignored `captures/`; review the source terms. Actual apartment paths may use a different video filename; use `captures/apartment_case_01/source_manifest.json`. Validate fresh results with `python -m pipeline.validation outputs\\<run>\\result.json`.

All capture media and generated point clouds/previews remain local under ignored `captures/` and `outputs/`. Commit the small report/code/manifest artifacts only; do not force-add image or video files.
